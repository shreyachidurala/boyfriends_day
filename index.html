To my favourite person...

Dear love,

Happy Boyfriend’s Day to my favourite person. I honestly don’t know what I would do without you. You’ve become such an important part of my life, I’m so grateful for every little moment, every laugh, every conversation and even the silly fights we have. You have this way of making me feel loved and special without even trying, and that means more to me than you know. I may not always say it perfectly but I love you so much and I’m really lucky to call you mine.

And honestly, some of my favourite things about us are all those little moments that only we understand — our stupid inside jokes, random conversations, laughing over things that wouldn’t even make sense to anyone else and all the memories we’ve made together. I love how even the smallest moments with you somehow become my favourite memories. From our silly teasing to those late-night talks and the little things you do that I secretly adore, everything feels so much more special because it’s with you. I hope we keep making a million more memories and having jokes that nobody else will ever understand.

With love ♡
